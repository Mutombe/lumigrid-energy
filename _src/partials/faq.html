Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to what Zimbabwean homeowners and businesses ask us most about going solar.

How big a system do I need for my home?{{> i-caret-down}}

Lights, TV, Wi-Fi and a fridge run well on 3 kVA with a 5 kWh battery. A borehole pump or home office usually means 5 to 8 kVA. A free load check sets the exact size.

Will my system work during load-shedding?{{> i-caret-down}}

Yes. A hybrid system switches to battery within milliseconds, so most appliances never flicker. Panels run the house by day and the battery takes over at night.

Lithium or gel batteries, which is better?{{> i-caret-down}}

Lithium (LiFePO4) for most clients. It costs more upfront but lasts several times longer, needs no maintenance and ends up cheaper over the life of the system.

How long does an installation take?{{> i-caret-down}}

A home system takes one to two days once the kit is on site. Farm, school and commercial projects run in phases on a timeline we agree with you first.

Do you offer warranties and after-sales support?{{> i-caret-down}}

Yes. Equipment carries manufacturer warranties and we guarantee our workmanship, with monitoring, maintenance visits and a support line after handover.

Can you power a borehole pump with solar?{{> i-caret-down}}

Yes. The pump runs straight off the panels by day or through your hybrid system. Add a storage tank and you have water around the clock.
