Our story

Built on a simple idea: power you can count on.

Load-shedding meant candles, defrosting fridges and climbing fuel bills. Lumigrid was founded to end that with properly engineered solar.

We assess your load, design the system, supply the equipment, install and commission it, then support it for years.
